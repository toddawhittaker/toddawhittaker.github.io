I'm the Assistant Dean for Computing Sciences and Mathematics at Franklin University, where I also serve as Program Chair for the Master of Science in Information Systems. I have more than 30 years of experience in computing-related fields. Before Franklin, I was an Associate Professor at DeVry University, a researcher at Battelle Memorial Institute, and a UNIX Systems Administrator at the University of Akron.

As assistant dean, I oversee academic operations, program health, and faculty engagement across Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, and Cloud Computing. I lead work on curriculum innovation, strategic partnerships, and connecting students with careers, and I've designed and launched five degree programs at Franklin. My research is in computing education and information security.

Outside of work, I enjoy time with my family, camping, hiking, gardening, and leading a small home group Bible study.
