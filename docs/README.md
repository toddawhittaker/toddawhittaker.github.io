Dr. Todd Whittaker is the Assistant Dean for Computing Sciences and Mathematics at Franklin University, where he also serves as Program Chair for the Master of Science in Information Systems. He has more than 30 years of experience in computing-related fields. Before Franklin, he was an Associate Professor at DeVry University, a Software Engineer at Battelle Memorial Institute, and a UNIX Systems Administrator at the University of Akron.

As assistant dean, Todd oversees academic operations, program health, and faculty engagement across Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, and Cloud Computing. He leads work on curriculum innovation, strategic partnerships, and connecting students with careers.

His expertise spans software development, systems and network administration, cybersecurity, and technology education. Outside of work, he enjoys time with his family, camping, hiking, gardening, and leading a small home group Bible study.
