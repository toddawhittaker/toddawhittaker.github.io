Dr. Todd Whittaker is the Assistant Dean for Computing Sciences and Mathematics at Franklin University, where he also serves as Program Chair for the Master of Science in Information Systems. He has more than 30 years of experience in computing-related fields. Before Franklin, he was an Associate Professor at DeVry University, a researcher at Battelle Memorial Institute, and a UNIX Systems Administrator at the University of Akron.

As assistant dean, Todd oversees academic operations, program health, and faculty engagement across Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, and Cloud Computing. He leads work on curriculum innovation, strategic partnerships, and connecting students with careers, and he has designed and launched five degree programs at Franklin. His research is in computing education and information security.

Outside of work, he enjoys time with his family, camping, hiking, gardening, and leading a small home group Bible study.

## Writing

Todd writes [Layer 8 Learning](https://layer8learning.substack.com/){:target="_blank"}, where he thinks in public about computing, higher education, leadership, and the changing shape of professional work. Recent essays look at how generative AI changes what student work can show, which technical fundamentals still matter when AI helps build software, and how colleges protect the value of their own degrees.
